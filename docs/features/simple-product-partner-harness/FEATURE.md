# Simple Product Partner Harness

## Goal

Keep the harness as a proactive product and engineering partner while reducing duplicated policy, specification bulk, and ambiguous completion claims.

## Behavior

- `AGENTS.md` remains a compact router for authority, lanes, routing, completion truth, safety, and handoff; detailed shaping, proof, and execution procedures live only in their owning skills and harness docs.
- The public harness is organized around seven durable pillars: understand first, high-value questions, bounded enrichment, lean authority, simple modular architecture, real-lifecycle proof, and truthful completion.
- Shaping accepts rough or detailed, single or multiple product input; asks only material grouped questions with recommendations; and creates only earned app, architecture, feature, proof, and queue artifacts.
- Proof selects relevant real usage states, including existing persisted data and visible read-back after refresh, remount, reopen, or restart. It does not mechanically apply every risk to every feature.
- Completion distinguishes an isolated source candidate from the named local or deployed runtime the user consumes. When an active runtime is the accepted consumption target, source proof is intermediate: the feature cannot reach `done` until that exact runtime is activated and validated.
- Safe local rebuild, restart, and verification continue autonomously. Approval-gated deployment or an exhausted external dependency keeps the feature incomplete and moves it to `blocked` with the exact required action; it is never converted into source-only completion.
- Preflight and one final evaluator remain fresh, relevance-bounded, read-only challenges. One accountable parent still owns serial implementation and repair.

## Constraints

- Prefer deleting duplication and moving judgment to the smallest owner over adding stages, reviewers, schemas, or checklists.
- Preserve realistic captured proof, final-candidate freshness, queue semantics, safety approvals, and explicit implementation authorization.
- Do not promise deterministic product judgment from static policy checks.

## Non-Goals

- Removing preflight, proof capture, or final evaluation.
- Proving a deployment when no deployed runtime is part of the accepted consumption target.
- Adding active-runtime receipts, hashes, environment registries, or deployment automation.
