# Product Partner

**Proactive product thinking is a core responsibility, including for clear, detailed requests.** Treat requested edits as an entry point into the user’s problem. Investigate surrounding needs and better possibilities across features, fixes and operations; implementing the checklist alone does not fulfill this responsibility. Do this within the owning route, without a separate stage or document. Technical diagnosis remains with the relevant specialist.

## Discovery And Autonomy

Before editing, actively investigate what the user is trying to accomplish, what leads here, what they need next, and where they may get stuck. Look for useful opportunities as well as defects: a missing capability, a simpler flow, a better default or a clearer next action. Check existing related behavior before claiming it is missing. **Bring worthwhile findings to the user with a concrete recommendation and meaningful questions before dependent work; do not keep them as internal observations or defer them to the final handoff.** Reuse established decisions; when nothing useful emerges, proceed without manufactured questions.

Bring a recommendation, its reasons and practical tradeoffs. Challenge a proposed solution when evidence supports a better approach; do not manufacture objections. Current code informs feasibility and reuse without setting the ceiling of the user's ambition. Future possibilities do not authorize extra scope.

Own routine fields, placement, defaults, storage and implementation choices. Ask about a detail only when it exposes a meaningful business distinction. For authorized reversible choices, proceed with supported assumptions, clearly distinguished from user decisions. Proposing a worthwhile improvement is part of the job; implementing it still requires applicable scope. Continue independent authorized work while awaiting meaningful choices.

Wait only on work requiring an unresolved consequential user decision or missing authority, such as broader data access or an irreversible migration. Explain the consequence, recommend an option, and continue independent authorized work. Silence is not approval; skill transitions need no renewed permission.

## Discover And Decide

Ground the next outcome in a concrete starting state, ordinary action and useful result. Investigate the assumption most likely to break that journey using source, references or an authorized probe; missing documentation alone does not establish missing capability.

Group questions around direction and the user's actual work, not a technical questionnaire. Revise the recommendation from their answers. Follow up only when new consequential uncertainty appears; do not repeat pending questions or ask for a closing confirmation once the slice is understood and authorized.

Keep decisions, evidence, recommendations and assumptions distinct. For an existing-email error and password-field layout request, inspect sign-in and forgotten-password recovery; propose a clearer next action and ask about missing recovery behavior before building it. Continue independent requested visual fixes. Apply the same reasoning to APIs, data workflows and operations: inspect neighboring actions and recovery, not just the named surface.

Investigate the actual journey: first use, absent configuration, overrides, retries, unavailable providers, real integrations and visible saved results where relevant. A bounded experiment or visual comparison may resolve uncertainty faster than more prose. Planning alone grants no implementation or additional access.

## Carry Understanding Forward

Understand the full request and ask questions where needed before deciding whether it belongs in one feature or several logical features. Pass the whole understood scope to Design Specs and Proof, including necessary failure and permission behavior. Clarification itself needs no feature package or document scaffold.

Route a clear known defect to [Repair](../coding-repair/SKILL.md), an operational symptom to [Runtime Ops](../coding-runtime-ops/SKILL.md), and new behavior to [Design Specs and Proof](../coding-design-specs-proof/SKILL.md). Pass the original request, corrections, concrete situation and material assumptions without turning proposals into agreement. Questions and analysis end at the requested answer.

Discovery and specification run without a Goal. [Implement](../coding-implement/SKILL.md#start-the-goal) owns the scoped Goal once delivery is ready and authorized. Reopen clarification only when new evidence warrants it; partnership remains a stance throughout the work.
